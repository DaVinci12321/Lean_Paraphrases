Let $A,B,C \in (0,\pi)$ with $A+B+C=\pi$ be the angles of a triangle, and let $a=4$ and $b=6$ be the lengths of the sides opposite $A$ and $B$; if the area satisfies $\tfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C = 6\sqrt{2}$, prove that $C = \tfrac{\pi}{4}$ or $C = \tfrac{3\pi}{4}$. Show that the answer is $45^{\circ}$ or $135^{\circ}$.
A sealed container in the shape of a right circular cone has height $12$ inches and base radius $5$ inches; when the cone is held with its vertex down and its base horizontal, the liquid inside has depth $9$ inches, and when the cone is held with its vertex up and its base horizontal, the depth of the liquid equals $m - n\sqrt[3]{p}$ inches for positive integers $m, n, p$ with $p$ not divisible by the cube of any prime. Find $m + n + p$. Prove that the answer is $52$.
Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \geq 2$. Prove that $\sum \frac{1}{ab} = \frac{1}{2}$, where the sum is taken over all pairs of positive integers $(a,b)$ with $\gcd(a,b)=1$, $a < b \leq n$, and $a+b > n$.
Let $x$ be a positive real number such that $\cos(\tan^{-1}(x)) = x$; prove that $x^2 = \frac{-1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$.
Find all pairs $(z,r)$ with $z \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $r \in \mathbb{Q}$ such that $2^{z}+2=r^{2}$, and show that the set of all such pairs is exactly $\left\{\left(-2,-\frac{3}{2}\right),\left(-2,\frac{3}{2}\right),(1,-2),(1,2)\right\}$.
Let $p, q \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $q = \frac{2}{5}p$ and $pq = 90$; show that $p = 15$.
Let $y:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ satisfy $y(t)=y_0e^{kt}$ for all $t\in\mathbb{R}$, where $y_0,k\in\mathbb{R}$ are constants; given $y(4)=100$ and $y(8)=10$, prove that $y(12)=1$, that is, the answer is $1$.
Let $a,b,c$ be integers with $a,b,c \geq 1$ such that $a^{b} \mid b^{c}$ and $a^{c} \mid c^{b}$. Prove that $a^{2} \mid bc$.
Find the number of positive integers $p$ satisfying $-1<\sqrt{p}-\sqrt{100}<1$. Show that the answer is $39$.
Let $f:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a function defined on all non-negative integers such that for every integer $n>1$ there exists a prime $p$ with $p\mid n$ and $f(n)=f(n/p)-f(p)$, and suppose $f(2^{2014})+f(3^{2015})+f(5^{2016})=2013$; prove that $f(2014^{2})+f(2015^{3})+f(2016^{5})=\frac{49}{3}$.
Prove that $\sum_{k=1}^{4} \binom{10}{k}\binom{10-k}{5-k} = 7560$, i.e. the number of ways to choose a committee of $5$ people from $10$ distinguishable people together with a nonempty set of $k$ committee leaders inside it, where $1 \le k \le 4$, is $7560$. Show that the answer is $7560$.
For $x \in \mathbb{R}$, prove that $\displaystyle\int \frac{x+2}{\sqrt{6+10x+25x^2}}\,dx = \frac{1}{25}\sqrt{6+10x+25x^2}+\frac{9}{25}\ln\left(1+5x+\sqrt{1+(5x+1)^2}\right)+C$, i.e. show that the answer is $\frac{1}{25}\sqrt{6+10x+25x^2}+\frac{9}{25}\ln\left(1+5x+\sqrt{1+(5x+1)^2}\right)+C$.
Let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be given by $f(x)=2x^2\sin x$; determine its $27$th derivative $f^{(27)}$. Prove that the answer is $f^{(27)}(x)=1404\cos x-2x^2\cos x-108x\sin x$ for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$.
Prove that for a real number $x$, the equation $x^2 - 2|x| - 3 = 0$ holds if and only if $x = -3$ or $x = 3$. Show that the answer is $x=-3 \lor x=3$.
Let $\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta$ be real numbers with $0<\alpha<\beta<\pi/2$, $\gamma\in(0,\pi/2)$, $\delta\in(0,\pi/2)$, $\tan\gamma=\frac{\tan\alpha+\tan\beta}{2}$ and $\frac{1}{\cos\delta}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{\cos\alpha}+\frac{1}{\cos\beta}\right)$; prove that $\gamma<\delta$.
Let $a,b,c \in \mathbb{R}^{+}$ with $abc \leq 1$. Prove that $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c} \geq 1 + \frac{6}{a+b+c}$.
Let $A,B\in\mathbb{Z}$ and set $M_1=\{x^2+Ax+B \mid x\in\mathbb{Z}\}$ and, for $C\in\mathbb{Z}$, $M_2=\{2x^2+2x+C \mid x\in\mathbb{Z}\}$; prove that there exists $C\in\mathbb{Z}$ such that $M_1\cap M_2=\varnothing$.
Prove that $\arctan\frac{1}{3}+\arctan\frac{1}{5}+\arctan\frac{1}{7}+\arctan\frac{1}{8}=\frac{\pi}{4}$.
A drawer contains $2$ red, $2$ black, $2$ white and $x$ blue socks, where $x$ is a positive integer, and two socks are drawn at random without replacement; given that $\frac{2\cdot 1+2\cdot 1+2\cdot 1+x(x-1)}{(6+x)(5+x)}=\frac{1}{5}$, determine $x$. Show that the answer is $4$.
Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n \ge 4$, and let $a_1,\dots,a_n$ and $b_1,\dots,b_n$ be nonnegative real numbers with $a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_n=b_1+b_2+\cdots+b_n>0$; find the maximum possible value of $\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n a_i(a_i+b_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^n b_i(a_i+b_i)}$, and prove that the answer is $n-1$.
Let $I:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be given by $I(x)=\int_{-0.6}^{-0.2}\left(2.1t^{5}+1.0\sin t\right)e^{x\left(-1.1t^{2}+1.5\cos t\right)}\,dt$; prove that $\lim_{x\to\infty} \dfrac{I(x)}{-0.27\,e^{1.43x}/x}=1$, i.e. show that the answer is $I(x)\approx-\frac{0.27e^{1.43x}}{x}$.
Prove that $\cos\frac{\pi}{7}-\cos\frac{2\pi}{7}+\cos\frac{3\pi}{7}=\frac{1}{2}$.
Let $c \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $f(x) = |x-20| + |x+18|$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$; prove that there exists exactly one real $x$ with $f(x) = x + c$ if and only if $c = 18$. Show that the answer is $18$.
Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_n$ be positive real numbers, and for each $k$ with $1\le k\le n$ define $A_k=\frac{a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_k}{k}$; prove that $\left(A_n-\frac{1}{A_n}\right)^2\le\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(a_i-\frac{1}{a_i}\right)^2$.
Let $x,y \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $x < y < 0$; prove that $\sqrt{(x-y)^2} + \sqrt{(x+y)^2} = -2x$.
Let $x$ be a real number satisfying $x^{3}+4x=8$. Prove that $x^{7}+64x^{2}=128$.
Let $x,y,z,w\in\mathbb{R}$ satisfy $2x+y+z+w=1$, $x+3y+z+w=2$, $x+y+4z+w=3$, and $x+y+z+5w=25$; find $w$. Show that the answer is $\frac{11}{2}$.
Let $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that $\gcd(12n+5,\,9n+4)=1$.
Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $x \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $x \geq n$. Prove that $x + \frac{n}{x} \geq n+1$, and that equality holds if and only if $x = n$; show that the answer is $x=n$.
Let $p,q,r$ be prime numbers and let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfy $pq+qr+rp = 12k+1$; show that the answer is $k=5$ with $(p,q,r)$ one of $(2,3,11)$, $(2,11,3)$, $(3,11,2)$, $(3,2,11)$, $(11,2,3)$, $(11,3,2)$.
Prove that the least value attained by $\frac{9x^2\sin^2 x + 4}{x\sin x}$ over all real $x$ with $0 < x < \pi$ is $12$; that is, show that the answer is $12$.
Let $x,y,z\in\mathbb{R}$ satisfy $0<x<\pi/2$, $0<y<\pi/2$ and $0<z<\pi/2$; prove that $\frac{\pi}{2}+2\sin x\cos y+2\sin y\cos z>\sin 2x+\sin 2y+\sin 2z$.
Let $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_{10}\in\left[0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ satisfy $\sin^2 x_1+\sin^2 x_2+\cdots+\sin^2 x_{10}=1$; prove that $3(\sin x_1+\sin x_2+\cdots+\sin x_{10})\le \cos x_1+\cos x_2+\cdots+\cos x_{10}$.
Determine the set of all real numbers $x$ satisfying $-4(x-5)(x+4)(x-1)\le 0$, and show that this set equals $[-4,1]\cup[5,\infty)$.
Let $a,b,c,d \ge 0$ be real numbers with $a+b+c+d=1$; prove that $bcd+cda+dab+abc \le \frac{1}{27}+\frac{176}{27}abcd$.
Find the number of ordered pairs of integers $(a,b)$ satisfying $a^2+b^2<16$, $a^2+b^2<8a$, and $a^2+b^2<8b$. Show that the answer is $6$.
Determine all quadruples $(x,y,z,t)$ of positive integers satisfying $20^x + 14^{2y} = (x+2y+z)^{zt}$. Prove that the answer is $(x,y,z,t)=(1,1,3,1)$.
Let $n \geq 0$ be an integer and let $a_0, a_1, \ldots, a_n$ be real numbers; show that there exists $k \in \{0, 1, \ldots, n\}$ such that $\sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i x^i \leq \sum_{i=0}^{k} a_i$ for all real $x \in [0,1]$.
Let $n = \binom{200}{100}$. Show that $61$ is the greatest element of the set of primes $p$ with $p \mid n$ and $p < 100$. Prove that the answer is $61$.
Show that the minimum value of $\sqrt{58-42x}+\sqrt{149-140\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}$ over all real $x$ with $-1\le x\le 1$ is attained and equals $\sqrt{109}$; that is, prove that the answer is $\sqrt{109}$.
